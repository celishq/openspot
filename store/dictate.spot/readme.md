Turns your speech into text: press your Dictate key, speak, and press it again to paste the text where you type. Its sheet lists your past texts, and Return copies one.

It needs the speech engine whisper-cpp. Install it in Terminal with `brew install whisper-cpp`, or press Install speech engine in its sheet to copy that command. At its first use it downloads a speech model of 1.6 GB. It needs the microphone. Your speech stays on your Mac: it sends nothing but the download request.
