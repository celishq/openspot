Turns your speech into text: press your Dictate key, speak, and press it again to paste the text where you type. Its sheet lists your past texts, and Return copies one.

It needs the speech engine, which you install with Homebrew as whisper-cpp, and a speech model of 1.6 GB, which it downloads at its first use. It needs the microphone. Your speech stays on your Mac: it sends nothing but the download request.
