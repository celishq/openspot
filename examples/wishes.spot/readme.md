# Wishes

Wishes keeps the things you want to buy, each with its name, its picture and its price.

- **Add a wish.** Press Return on **Add wish** and paste a product link. Wishes reads the page and shows the name, the
  price and the type that it found. Change what is wrong, and press Return to keep the wish.
- **Open a wish.** Press Return on it, and its page opens in your browser.
- **Group the list.** `⌘G` switches between product types, such as Books and Gadgets, and price ranges.
- **Share the list.** `⌘S` copies the list as text under price ranges, and `⇧⌘S` under product types. Paste it into
  any message.
- **Fix a wish.** `⌘T` changes its type, `⌘E` its name and price, and `⌘⌫` removes it.

Some shops hide the price from programs. The form then leaves the price empty, and you type it.
