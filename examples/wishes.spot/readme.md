# Wishes

Wishes keeps the things you want to buy, each with its name, its picture and its price.

- **Add a wish.** Paste a product link into the search, and press Return on **Add wish**. Wishes reads the page and
  fills in the name, the price, the type and the picture, which you can change. The arrows move between the fields and
  the Save button, and `⌘↓` jumps to Save. Return moves on from a field and opens the list of types on Type. Press
  Return on Save, or `⌘⏎` anywhere, to keep the wish.
- **Add notes.** Notes are yours to fill, such as a size or a colour. They show in the wish's line and on a line of their own in the shared
  list.
- **Add or delete a type.** In the list of types, choose **Add type…**, type its name, such as Plants, and press
  Return: the wish comes back with it. To delete a type you added, press `⌘⌫` on it in the same list, then save the
  wish; its wishes move to Other. Books, Clothes and the other types that come with Wishes always stay.
- **Open a wish.** Press Return on it, and its page opens in your browser.
- **Edit a wish.** `⌘E` opens the same form, with its link, name, price, type and picture. A wish that has no
  picture or price reads its page again for them. `⌘⌫` removes a wish.
- **Share the list.** Press Return on **Share list**, at the bottom, and paste the list into any message.
- **Keep the list where you want.** Your wishes are one file, `Wishes.md`, that you can open and edit in any app, such
  as Obsidian or TextEdit. Each type is a `##` heading, and each wish a line with its link, its price and its notes.
  Choose **Keep in** in the Settings of Wishes to pick its folder, such as one in iCloud Drive: the list moves there,
  or Wishes reads the `Wishes.md` that the folder already holds. Until you choose, it stays in the folder of Wishes.
- **Group the list.** Choose **Group by** in the Settings of Wishes, `⌘⏎` on its card: by type, such as Books and
  Gadgets, or by price range. The shared list follows it.

Some shops, such as those behind Cloudflare, don't let programs read their pages. Then Wishes reads the same page from
an open tab of your browser: Chrome, Safari, Arc, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi or Opera. macOS asks once to let Openspot
control the browser. With no such tab, Wishes takes the name from the link, and you type the price.

Wishes drops tracking words, such as `utm_source`, from each link.
