Puts the front tab of your browser in the panel's field, with its title. Open the panel over a browser that Openspot reads, such as Safari, Chrome or Arc, press Tab, and pick a spot that takes a link, such as Links.

It reads the tab only when Tab picks its chip. Until you allow Openspot to read the browser, the field says so, and Return opens the Attachments spot, where you allow it. [Hand what you work on to a spot](../../MANUAL.md#hand-what-you-work-on-to-a-spot) says how to add a browser.
