Answers sums, units, currencies and dates as you type them in the search, such as `2 * 7`, `5 km in miles` or `today + 3 weeks`.

The answer shows as a large card: the answer in big numbers, over the sum as it was read, small and faint. Press Enter to copy the answer. Currency rates come from the network once a day, and the card names their day when they are old.
