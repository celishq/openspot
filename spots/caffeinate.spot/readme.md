Keeps the Mac awake. Open its sheet and pick how long: 30 minutes to 8 hours, or until you turn it off. While it is on,
the card shows the time left, and Turn off is the first row. Quitting Openspot turns it off.

Turn on **Work with the lid closed** to keep the Mac working with its lid closed or its screen locked. The display then
sleeps and locks as usual. The first time, macOS asks you to turn on Openspot in Login Items & Extensions, which lets
Openspot's power helper keep the Mac awake. While it is on, the Mac never sleeps, even closed in a bag, so it can grow
hot and drain its battery.

It runs the `caffeinate` command of macOS while it is on, and `openspot power lid-closed` when you work with the lid
closed.
