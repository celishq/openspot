Shows a colour that a row holds, such as `#ff3b30`, `rgb(255 59 48)` or `hsl(3, 100%, 59%)`, in the row's preview as hex, RGBA and HSL. Press Space on a copied colour in the Timeline sheet to see it, and `⌘K` to copy it as Hex, RGBA or HSL.

It runs inside the app, only when a preview or a `⌘K` menu shows such a row, and stays off the grid.
