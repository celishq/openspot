Puts what you select in the panel's field: the files you select in Finder, or the text you select in any other app. Select something, open the panel, press Tab, and pick a spot that takes it, such as Shortcuts for files or Snippets for text.

It reads the selection when the panel opens: Finder's through Automation, and the text of other apps through Accessibility, which you allow on its page in Settings. Apps that do not share their selection give nothing, and then no chip shows.
