Puts what you copied in the last 2 minutes in the panel's field: a link as a link, anything else as text. Copy something, open the panel, press Tab, and pick a spot that takes it.

It reads the clipboard only when the panel opens. It counts the 2 minutes from the moment Openspot first sees the copy, at the latest the next open of the panel, so a copy made while Openspot was not looking shows once more at that open.
