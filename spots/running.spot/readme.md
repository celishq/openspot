Shows how much you use each spot and what it costs, over the last 14 days. The chart puts each spot by its acts a day
across and its CPU a day up, so a spot that costs much and that you rarely use stands out at the top left.

Below the chart, each spot has a bar for each day of acts, under the title of its state: used, failing, slow to answer,
or unused for 14 days. An act is something you did with a spot, such as opening it or picking one of its rows. A spot
that a card of other spots shows, or that another spot starts, counts as used. A card of other spots, such as System, has
its own group and shows what the spots on it cost.

It reads the stats that Openspot keeps on your Mac. It sends nothing over the network.

Return on a row, or a click on a dot, opens the page of that spot: its acts of each day, its CPU a day, how fast 95 in 100
of its calls answer, its peak memory, what it costs while the panel is hidden, its failures, its reads and its uses, and the spots it shows, is shown on, starts and is started by.
